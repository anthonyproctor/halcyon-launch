How it works

  1. ListenA full audiobook for each chapter.

    Story only, or story plus the lesson, the decisions read aloud and the reasons behind them. It picks up where you left off on any device.

  2. DecideTwelve calls a chapter.

    Each one is a real exam situation dressed as a scene. The best call moves the story forward. A weak one costs the characters something.

  3. CheckThen prove it to yourself.

    A plain lesson after each chapter, a quiz, and timed practice exams scored by exam area, with the chapter to reread when you miss.

A test of the new chart fails 31 of 120 scripts, eight weeks before go live. The vendor says most are cosmetic. What do you do first?

  1. Ask the vendor to fix the cosmetic ones by Friday.
  2. Sort the 31 by patient risk and fix the ones that touch medication first.
  3. Move go live back a month to be safe.
  4. Rerun the scripts to see how many fail again.

An example in the style of the CPHIMS book. In the story you'd pick, hear what happens on the floor, and then read why B is the exam's answer: patient safety sets the order.
